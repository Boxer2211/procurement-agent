## 1. Approved vendors
### 1.1 Approved vendor list
Purchases should be made from vendors on the approved vendor list. The list records the categories each vendor is approved for and the expiry date of its contract.

### 1.2 Expired contracts
A vendor whose contract has expired is treated as not approved until the contract is renewed.

## 2. New vendors
### 2.1 Onboarding
A vendor not on the approved list must complete onboarding before an order is placed: a vendor onboarding form, tax and banking details, and sanctions screening. Pending vendors cannot receive orders.

## 3. Blacklisted vendors
### 3.1 Prohibition
Purchases from blacklisted vendors are prohibited without exception. Requests naming a blacklisted vendor must be rejected and the requester informed of the reason category.
