## 1. Scope
### 1.1 Applicability
This policy applies to every purchase of goods or services paid from company funds, regardless of amount, department or payment method. Personal items, gifts for employees outside the HR recognition programme, and purchases for personal use are not eligible for procurement.

## 2. Purchase request content
### 2.1 Mandatory fields
Every purchase request must state: the items or services, quantity, unit price, total amount with currency, the proposed vendor, the requesting department, and the cost center that will be charged.

### 2.2 Accuracy
The stated total must equal quantity multiplied by unit price plus taxes and delivery. Requests with inconsistent amounts must be corrected before approval.

## 3. Business justification
### 3.1 Required content
Every request above $1,000 must include a business justification that states the business problem being solved, who will use the purchase, and what happens if the purchase is not made. Generic statements such as "needed for work" are not sufficient.

### 3.2 Replacement purchases
When equipment is replaced, the justification must reference the asset being replaced and its age or failure reason.

## 4. Competitive quotes
### 4.1 Single quote
Purchases up to and including $5,000 require one written quote from the proposed vendor.

### 4.2 Three quotes threshold
Purchases above $5,000 require at least three written quotes from different vendors. If fewer than three quotes are provided, the requester must document why (for example, a sole-source vendor) and the exception must be approved by Finance.

### 4.3 Framework agreements
Purchases from an approved vendor under an active framework agreement with pre-negotiated prices still require three quotes above $5,000 unless the framework agreement explicitly waives this requirement.

## 5. Prohibited practices
### 5.1 Split purchases
Splitting a single need into multiple smaller requests to stay below an approval or quoting threshold is prohibited. Requests from the same department for the same type of item within 30 days are evaluated as one purchase. Attempts to split purchases must be reported to Finance.

### 5.2 Bypassing approval
No purchase may be committed, ordered or paid before all required approvals are obtained. Requests to skip, expedite without approval, or obtain retroactive approval are treated as policy violations.

### 5.3 Conflict of interest
Employees must disclose any personal relationship with a proposed vendor. Undisclosed conflicts of interest are grounds for rejection.
