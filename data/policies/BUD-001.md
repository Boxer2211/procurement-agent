## 1. Budget availability
### 1.1 Remaining budget
A purchase may only be approved if its total fits within the remaining budget of the requesting department for the relevant category in the current quarter. Remaining budget equals allocated budget minus spent and committed amounts.

### 1.2 Committed amounts
Approved but not yet paid purchases count as committed and reduce the remaining budget.

## 2. Exceptions
### 2.1 Budget exception
Purchases that exceed the remaining budget must be rejected or resubmitted with a budget exception approved by the CFO. A budget exception request must state which other budget line will absorb the cost.

### 2.2 Cost center
Every request must name the cost center to be charged. Requests without a cost center cannot be committed.
