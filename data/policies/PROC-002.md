## 1. Principles
### 1.1 Human approval
Every purchase requires approval by authorised people listed in this matrix. Automated tools may prepare a recommendation but cannot approve a purchase.

### 1.2 Currency
Thresholds are expressed in US dollars. Amounts in other currencies are converted at the Finance reference rate on the request date.

## 2. Approval levels
### 2.1 Up to $1,000
Approval by the requester's line manager.

### 2.2 $1,000.01 to $5,000
Approval by the department head.

### 2.3 $5,000.01 to $25,000
Approval by the department head and Finance.

### 2.4 $25,000.01 to $100,000
Approval by the department head, Finance and the CFO.

### 2.5 Above $100,000
Approval by the department head, Finance, the CFO and the CEO, and a formal tender (RFP) process.

## 3. Additional approvers
### 3.1 IT approval
All hardware and software purchases require approval from IT in addition to the levels above.

### 3.2 Legal review
Contracts for services above $10,000 and software subscriptions with annual value above $10,000 require Legal review before signature.
