## 1. Scope
### 1.1 Covered equipment
This standard covers laptops, desktops, monitors, peripherals, mobile phones and networking equipment issued to employees.

## 2. Laptop profiles
### 2.1 Specification required
Every hardware request must include the model or a specification (CPU, RAM, storage, screen size) so IT can verify it matches a standard profile.

### 2.2 Developer profile
Engineering staff may receive a developer laptop with at least 32 GB RAM and 1 TB SSD. The maximum unit price for a developer laptop is $2,000 excluding tax.

### 2.3 Standard profile
Non-engineering staff receive a standard laptop with 16 GB RAM and 512 GB SSD. The maximum unit price for a standard laptop is $1,300 excluding tax.

### 2.4 Exceptions
Devices above the profile price cap require written approval from the IT director with a technical justification.

## 3. Lifecycle
### 3.1 Refresh cycle
Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Early replacement requires a failure report or a documented change of role.

### 3.2 New hires
Equipment for new hires must reference the hiring request or start date.

## 4. Monitors and peripherals
### 4.1 Monitors
Each employee may have up to two monitors. The maximum unit price for a monitor is $600.
