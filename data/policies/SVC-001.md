## 1. Scope
### 1.1 Covered services
This policy covers contractors, consultants, agencies, outsourced development, training providers and other professional services.

## 2. Required documents
### 2.1 Statement of work
Every services purchase requires a statement of work (SOW) that defines deliverables, timeline, acceptance criteria and pricing model.

### 2.2 Confidentiality
The vendor must sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before receiving any company information.

### 2.3 Master services agreement
Engagements above $10,000 require a signed master services agreement (MSA) reviewed by Legal.

## 3. Engagement limits
### 3.1 Duration
Engagements may not exceed 12 months. Extensions require a new request and approval.

### 3.2 Rates
Hourly or daily rates must be stated in the SOW and compared against at least two other providers for engagements above $5,000.
